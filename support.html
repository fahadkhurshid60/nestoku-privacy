Nestoku Support

Nestoku is a soft, pastel logic puzzle game for iOS. Place a cast of cute animals into their nests by following simple rules. Each puzzle has one solution that you can reach by logic alone. You don't need an account or sign-in, and you can play offline.

Contact

For help, feedback, or questions, email theorbito60@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop in Nestoku and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If you don't see a Restore button, or your purchases don't come back, email theorbito60@gmail.com and include your purchase receipt if you can.

How do I remove ads?

Buy the Ad-Free pack in the Shop. It is a one-time purchase that removes ads. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details.

Why do I see ads?

Ads, shown through Google AdMob, help keep Nestoku free to play. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads using Apple's tracking prompt. You can also change this later in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Puzzles work without an internet connection, and your progress is saved on your device. Some features, such as ads and purchases, need a connection.

How do I report a bug?

Email theorbito60@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, and app version, plus a short description of what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
