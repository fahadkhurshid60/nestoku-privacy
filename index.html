Nestoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Nestoku ("the app"), published by Muhammad Fahad Khurshid ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. Questions can be sent to khanghania667@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. You can play without creating an account. A player username you choose in the game is stored on your device and is not linked to your real identity.

Information collected by third-party services

The app uses these services, which may collect information automatically:

  • Google AdMob shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. On iOS we ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.
  • Google Firebase helps us run and measure in-app purchases and app performance. It may collect an app instance identifier, purchase events, in-app activity, device information, and crash or performance data. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple App Store processes all payments. We never receive your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was purchased so we can deliver it.

How information is used

  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore them
  • To show ads, and to measure and limit how often they appear
  • To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes and bugs

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can turn tracking on or off at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Buying an Ad-Free pack removes ads.
  • Deleting the app removes the game data stored on your device.
  • You can email us to ask about, or ask us to delete, any information we hold.

Children

Nestoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

Information collected by third-party services is kept according to their own policies. Game progress is stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
